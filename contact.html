Contact asiancoastline.com

Questions about the player, a broken stream, a guide correction, a privacy request, or a permission question — email is the reliable route.

Email

excavationstation@gmail.com

Please put “Asian Coastline” in the subject and allow a few business days for a reply. This is a small independent project, not a support desk.

What to include

  • For a player issue: the browser, the track title, and what you saw (an error, silence, a stall).
  • For a broken stream: the track title or the URL, and whether other tracks play.
  • For a guide correction: the page URL and the sentence you think is wrong.
  • For a privacy request: the email you used and what you want changed or removed.
  • For a permission request: the track, the intended use, the platform, and whether money is involved.

Do not send passwords, API keys, or private audio you do not want a stranger to hear.

Publisher and support

Justin Helmer · Canada. The site is part of the Excavationpro / LYGO workshop. Optional support through PayPal.me/ExcavationPro helps with hosting and time; it does not buy a custom track or a guarantee of support.

Response times and scope

This is a small independent project, not a 24-hour support desk. Most messages get a reply within a few business days. Player bugs and broken streams are prioritized; permission requests may take longer because they need a real decision rather than a quick answer.

If you are reporting a broken track, include the title and whether other tracks play. If you are reporting a guide error, include the URL and the sentence. A precise report is faster to fix than a screenshot with no context.
