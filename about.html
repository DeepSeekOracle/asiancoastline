What this site is

asiancoastline.com is the public listen domain for Justin Helmer / Excavationpro. The player carries the music catalog and the Eternal Haven audio in a browser, without an account. The catalog is the artist’s own, not a licensed subset, and the site states the listening terms plainly.

It is part of the same workshop as excavationpro.ca (music and essays), bpmfinder.ca (tempo tools), deepseekoracle.com (AI and creator guides), eternalhaven.ca (books and games), chatagent.ca (AI agent guides), and justinhelmer.ca (artist portal).

Why it exists

The first encounter with music should be a listen, not a negotiation. A platform feed is useful for discovery, but it is not the only room. This site keeps a direct listening room that survives platform changes, delistings, and recommendation algorithms. The player is intentionally simple: search, play, queue, radio, and a ledger, with no account wall.

How the catalog is maintained

Public streams are compressed listening copies. Masters stay offline in the vault. The ledger records a public snapshot of the catalog — titles, hashes, and metadata — so the public record can be checked even when a file moves. New work is added to the catalog deliberately; the site is curated, not an infinite upload feed.

Editorial standard

  • Original writing, not scraped or auto-spun text.
  • One real job per guide; no doorway pages or keyword stuffing.
  • Visible byline and update date on every guide.
  • Advertising is labelled and kept off the player, policy, and error pages.
  • Corrections are made in place and the update date changes.

Advertising and contact

This site may show Google AdSense advertising on editorial pages to support hosting and time. The player stays ad-free. The privacy policy explains cookies and consent. Contact: excavationstation@gmail.com with “Asian Coastline” in the subject.

How to verify the catalog

The player includes a ledger tab with the public catalog snapshot: hashes, titles, and metadata. The ledger is not a marketing claim; it is a reference you can compare against the files the player exposes. If a stream is missing, the ledger still shows what the catalog expected.

The site also publishes a sitemap, a privacy policy, and terms. Those are the boring pages that make the rest trustworthy. If something is wrong, the contact address reaches a person, not a ticket queue.
