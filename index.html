What this portal is

Independent free listening · Original work only

Major apps are useful, but a free browser portal keeps a direct path to original music. Streams here are derived for web listening from masters owned by the artist. You can explore without creating an account. Commercial rights stay with Justin Helmer / Excavationpro unless a separate license is granted.

The player page is intentionally powerful: large catalog, radio modes, live room links, and lattice notes. Publisher networks often prefer a calmer content front door — so this homepage focuses on clear writing, while /listen.html keeps the full system.

How free independent listening works

  1. Open the free player at /listen.html.
  2. Search or browse the catalog of original streams.
  3. Use radio / shuffle modes when you want continuous play.
  4. Optionally visit live rooms (Kick, Rumble, Twitch) linked from the player.
  5. Optional support links help hosting — never required to listen.

For tempo prep while you build a set, use the free BPM tool. For image→sound experiments, try the LYGO Resonance Engine.

Why keep masters offline?

Public streams are listening copies. Keep archival WAV/AIF masters in your own vault. That separation protects releases while still giving fans a free way to press play.
